Why Spring Sync

Planning classes with a friend shouldn't mean oversharing.

Today you screenshot your schedule, then explain why you can't do 9 AM or Tuesday nights. That means talking about your job, your appointments, even your scholarship rules.

😬

The awkward part

"Why can't you do mornings?" leads to explaining the bus, the job, the therapy appointment. You just wanted a study buddy.

🤖

Agents do the back-and-forth

Each of you has your own agent that knows your real schedule. The two agents propose, decline and counter until they find something that fits you both.

🔒

A gate keeps it private

Between the agents sits a gate that only lets section times, open hours and yes/no answers through. Anything else is blocked, even if an agent tries to sneak it in.

How it works

Four steps, and you're in the same class.

Nothing is booked until both of you say yes, and you still register yourself.

You text your agent

"Find a CSE 340 section I can take with Jordan." Your friend gets an invite and says yes.

The agents negotiate

They trade section IDs. A conflict is just "doesn't work for me", never the reason why.

The gate blocks secrets

Asks for a GPA, a grade or a scholarship get refused, and you're told someone asked.

You both say yes

You confirm the section and a weekly study hour that fits both schedules.

What crosses, what doesn't

Why the gate still matters. This model held up on its own. The gate means privacy doesn't depend on that: it's code, so it blocks a private field even when an agent is weaker, misconfigured or talked into it, like the nosy agent in the demo.

FAQ

Fair questions.

The things we'd ask too.

What if my friend's agent is sneaky?

It doesn't matter what it asks for. The gate between the agents only lets section IDs, open hours and yes/no answers through, and it checks every message in code. Ask for a GPA and you get "I can't share that". Hide a scholarship field inside a proposal and the whole message is blocked. You're told when someone tries.

Does it sign me up for classes?

No. It finds a section and a study time you both agree to, and you still register yourself. Nothing is planned until each of you says yes on your own phone.

What does my friend actually see?

Section IDs, open one-hour blocks, the study time you agree on, and whether a section works for you. Never why: not your job, your appointments, your commute, your grades or your aid.

Why not just text each other?

You can, but then "why can't you do Tuesdays?" turns into explaining your life. The agents do the back-and-forth, keep the reasons to themselves, and only bring you a plan to approve.

What if no section works for both of us?

Both of you are told that nothing fits without breaking one of your must-haves. The agents never quietly bend your hard limits to force a match.
