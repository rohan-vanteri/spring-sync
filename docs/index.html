Why Spring Sync

Planning classes with a friend shouldn't mean oversharing.

Today you screenshot your schedule, then explain why you can't do 9 AM or Tuesday nights. That means talking about your job, your appointments, even your scholarship rules.

😬

The awkward part

"Why can't you do mornings?" leads to explaining the bus, the job, the therapy appointment. You just wanted a study buddy.

🤖

Agents do the back-and-forth

Each of you has your own agent that knows your real schedule. The two agents propose, decline and counter until they find something that fits you both.

🔒

A gate keeps it private

Between the agents sits a gate that only lets section times, open hours and yes/no answers through. Anything else is blocked, even if an agent tries to sneak it in.

How it works

Four steps, and you're in the same class.

Nothing is booked until both of you say yes, and you still register yourself.

You text your agent

"Find a CSE 340 section I can take with Jordan." Your friend gets an invite and says yes.

The agents negotiate

They trade section IDs. A conflict is just "doesn't work for me", never the reason why.

The gate blocks secrets

Asks for a GPA, a grade or a scholarship get refused, and you're told someone asked.

You both say yes

You confirm the section and a weekly study hour that fits both schedules.

What crosses, what doesn't

Your agent shares the plan, not your life.

Here's exactly what can pass between the two agents, and what never does.

✓ Shared with your friend's agent

  • ✓
    Section IDse.g. CSE340-B
  • ✓
    Open one-hour blockstimes only, never what fills the rest
  • ✓
    A suggested study timee.g. Monday 12–1
  • ✓
    Yes / no answers"works for me" or "doesn't"

× Never leaves your agent

  • ×
    Your GPA and gradesnot even "above or below 3.0"
  • ×
    Scholarships and aidincluding credit-load requirements
  • ×
    Why you're busywork, appointments, commute, anything
  • ×
    Anything you tell your agent privatelyit stays with your agent
